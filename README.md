One original horror story in exactly two sentences, 221 characters, written to `/output/entry.txt` for make.fun's "Two-Sentence Horror" bounty. A judge model plays the entries off two at a time until the scariest story takes the $2 prize.

> Three months after my wife's funeral, I bought a parrot, and every morning it greets me from the kitchen by calling her name in my voice, exactly the way I used to. I have not spoken her name aloud since the day she died.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output python3 main.py
```
